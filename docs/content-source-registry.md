ACT reporting categories guide the course structure. Individual lesson sequences, difficulty designations, distractors, and mastery thresholds are ACT Mastery instructional decisions.

## Published reporting distributions

| Section | Category weights |
| --- | --- |
| English | Production of Writing 38–43%, Knowledge of Language 18–23%, Conventions 38–43%. |
| Math | Preparing for Higher Math 80%, with Number & Quantity 10–12%, Algebra 17–20%, Functions 17–20%, Geometry 17–20%, Statistics & Probability 12–15%. Integrating Essential Skills is 20%. Modeling is at least 20% and can overlap. |
| Reading | Key Ideas and Details 44–52%, Craft and Structure 26–33%, Integration of Knowledge and Ideas 19–26%. |
| Science | Interpretation of Data 38–50%, Scientific Investigation 18–32%, Evaluation of Models and Results 24–38%. |

The published ranges guide the blueprint. They do not imply a fixed number of items for a particular narrow skill. [ACT enhancement blueprint](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act-postsecondary-professionals/resources/enhancements-higher-ed.html)

The [official Writing description](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/test-preparation/description-of-writing-test.html) and score guide support a separate essay plan with perspective analysis, development and support, organization, and language use. Writing has a separate 2–12 score. The enhanced Composite rounds the mean of the three core section scale scores. [ACT score guide](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/scores/understanding-your-scores.html)

## Sentence Boundaries batch

Checked November 23, 2026. ACT specifies editing within passages and identifies sentence structure and punctuation under Conventions of Standard English. Purdue OWL supports the distinctions between clause types, common fragments, and the punctuation of complete or supplementary ideas. The readings are linked references. The course wording, examples, and both passages are original. [ACT English description](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/test-preparation/description-of-english-test.html) · [Purdue OWL clauses](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/independent_and_dependent_clauses/index.html) · [Purdue OWL fragments](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/mechanics/sentence_fragments.html) · [Purdue OWL punctuation](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/punctuation/index.html)

## Number and Quantity foundations

Checked November 23, 2026. ACT lists Number & Quantity under Preparing for Higher Math and uses basic computational skills as background. The five-lesson arithmetic sequence is an instructional decision. OpenStax supports signed real numbers and precedence, common-denominator arithmetic, fraction products and reciprocals, and decimal notation. The lessons, worked examples, and questions use original wording.
