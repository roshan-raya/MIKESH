The chapter CSV supplies the requirement and count for every topic. The official ACT blueprint uses reporting categories. The site's 71 titles are an instructional interpretation.

| Subject | Existing concentration | Priority gaps |
| --- | --- | --- |

Six chapters have four levels of instruction, including the new Exponents and Radicals course. | Review and expand the draft arithmetic pool, and exponent pools, then cover systems, full geometry and trigonometry, statistics and probability, modeling with figures and tables. Math has no hosted level 1 questions and only three level 5 questions. |
| English | Punctuation starter and four Sentence Boundaries lessons with two draft passages. Conventions are represented by a hosted topic with a blank reporting category alongside more specific mapped topics. | Review and release the staged sentence-boundary editing pool. A usable optional form and both diagnostic and mock pools are missing. |

Plan 8 prompts, 12 guided revision exercises and 8 annotated responses as editorial targets. |

Hosted English has only three questions attached to passage records. An inline excerpt in a prompt does not count as an attached passage. Number of items with attached records is not a passage length or quality measure. All official multiple-choice reporting families have headings or topic mappings. Their presence does not establish coverage of every underlying skill. The current app has practice in every multiple-choice subject, but it cannot supply an ACT-length form from approved material. The local English, Math and Reading mock drafts form one 50/45/36 core set by count. The mock publication guard and lack of approved material keep it unavailable. The chapter graphs are isolated, and English has four hosted profiles with its other 18 hosted items still unmapped, and Reading and Science have no complete profiles.

## Content quality limits

All inventory items supply a nonblank explanation. This is a presence check. Some explanations are short and subsection-specific reasoning still needs editorial review. Exact normalized same-subject prompt checks detected no duplicate families after reconciliation. Near-duplicate templates, prose inconsistencies, and semantic key defects require human inspection. All keys passed the structural choice check. The automated tests recompute selected Math batches. The audit does not certify the remaining Math, English, Reading, Science, or mock keys. All inventory rows claim original provenance. Twelve legacy items use a coarse starter label. Most have batch-level provenance, with item-specific source documentation and licensing review still incomplete.
