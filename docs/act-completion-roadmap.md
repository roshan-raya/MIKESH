# ACT completion roadmap

## Release gates

1. **P0 Security.** Replace the accepted legacy privileged credential and revoke the exposed credential and associated legacy signing configuration. Disable new-user signup and anonymous authentication as applicable, verify least-privilege access, and confirm recovery before rollout. Follow the [security cutover runbook](security-cutover.md).
2. **P1 Production.** Back up the hosted database and apply and verify the missing curriculum, course, editorial and focused retest capabilities. Recheck owner and cross-user boundaries in staging before publishing approved, version-matched items.
3. **P2 Curriculum.** Complete lesson and prerequisite coverage, review the current question batches, and assemble independent timed forms before presenting assessment readiness.
4. **P3 Polish.** Add alternate solutions, similar-question controls and source-level navigation once the content supports them.
